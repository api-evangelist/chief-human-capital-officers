# Chief Human Capital Officers (CHCO) Council

> The CHCO Council is the principal interagency forum of the U.S. federal government for
> coordinating human capital and human resources policy across departments and agencies.
> It was established by the Chief Human Capital Officers Act of 2002 (5 U.S.C. 1401-1402),
> is chaired by the Director of the U.S. Office of Personnel Management (OPM), and is
> vice-chaired by the Deputy Director for Management at OMB. The Council publishes policy
> memoranda, annual reports, working-group products and a member-agency roster.

## What this is, and what it is not

The CHCO Council is a **policy body, not an API provider**. As of 2026-09-05 it publishes
no API, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL contract, no SDK, no MCP server, no agent card and no
`/.well-known/` document. Everything below is a human-readable web page or a PDF. An agent
asking "how do I call the CHCO Council API" should be told plainly that there is not one.

The Council's former domain, `chcoc.gov`, now 301-redirects to `https://www.opm.gov/chcoc`;
its content lives inside the OPM website.

## Council resources

- [CHCO Council home](https://www.opm.gov/chcoc): Leadership, latest news, and entry points to every section below.
- [Published memos](https://www.opm.gov/chcoc/published-memos/): The Council's transmittal archive — government-wide HR policy memoranda issued to agency Chief Human Capital Officers. This is the Council's primary published output.
- [Member agencies](https://www.opm.gov/chcoc/member-agencies/): Roster of the departments and agencies represented on the Council.
- [Annual reports](https://www.opm.gov/chcoc/annual-reports/): Yearly reports on Council activity and federal human capital priorities.
- [CHCO Council news](https://www.opm.gov/chcoc/news/): Announcements and updates.
- [Legacy domain](https://www.chcoc.gov/): Redirects to the OPM-hosted Council site.

## Statutory and governance basis

- [5 U.S.C. § 1401 — Establishment of agency Chief Human Capital Officers](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/5/1401): The Chief Human Capital Officers Act of 2002, the Council's enabling statute.
- [USAGov agency entry](https://www.usa.gov/agencies/chief-human-capital-officers-council): The Council's entry in the official U.S. government agency directory.

## Parent agency and related federal HR surfaces

These belong to the U.S. Office of Personnel Management or to other federal programs, **not
to the CHCO Council**. They are listed because they are where federal human-capital data
actually is, and an agent looking for programmatic access should be pointed at them rather
than at the Council.

- [OPM](https://www.opm.gov): The Council's chair agency and web host.
- [OPM Developer](https://www.opm.gov/developer/): OPM's developer page — operating-status XML/JSON feeds and links to open data. These are OPM's feeds; the Council publishes none.
- [OPM Open Data](https://data.opm.gov): OPM's open-data platform, including federal workforce data downloads.
- [Performance management policy](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/performance-management/): OPM guidance the Council coordinates around.
- [USAJOBS](https://www.usajobs.gov/): The federal government's official employment site; the USAJOBS API is operated separately at data.usajobs.gov.

## Security and privacy

- [OPM Vulnerability Disclosure Policy](https://www.opm.gov/vulnerability-disclosure-policy/): The VDP governing this surface. Its scope explicitly names both `chcoc.gov` and `opm.gov`. Reports go to opm-vdp@submit.bugcrowd.com or https://bugcrowd.com/opm-vdp; scope questions to vulnerabilitydisclosure@opm.gov. No bounty is offered. Note that OPM does not serve an RFC 9116 `/.well-known/security.txt`, so this policy is not machine-discoverable.
- [OPM Privacy Program](https://www.opm.gov/privacy/): Privacy policy covering the OPM-hosted properties, including the Council site.
- [Accessibility (Section 508)](https://www.opm.gov/information-management/accessibility/)
